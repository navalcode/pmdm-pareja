The historical paired exercise credits Manuel Naval and Alejandro Ramírez; its original README section remains verbatim. Existing history and unrelated files are preserved. No blanket license is added over earlier coursework.

- The original photo and text stay as historical blobs and are not loaded by the interface. Their provenance is not inferred from repository ownership.
- Screenshots are actual local browser pixels with fictional values and empty password fields. Generated design references and formatting/research tooling are outside the public export.

The review does not claim that the original collaborators jointly authored the later refinements, or that this practice provides an authentication service.
